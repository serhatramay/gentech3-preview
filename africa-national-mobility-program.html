Contractual foundation

A contractually established 10-year national digital transportation and payments program under Gentech’s agreement with SANTACO acting through TaxiChoice.

The program is established under an existing agreement. Its contractual status does not represent a grant of government authority.

Johannesburg implementation

GENTECH CAPITAL HOLDINGS (PTY) LTD serves as Gentech Group’s Johannesburg-based operating and implementation company for the program.

This operating description does not assert that the company is the direct signatory or an exclusive executor.

PROGRAM DESIGN

Connected transport. Integrated payments.

The program architecture is designed to support these capabilities together with appropriately licensed banking, payment and technology partners.

Passenger access

Designed to support contactless and QR-based access, subject to product selection and integration.

Vehicle connectivity

Designed to support connected validators, terminal communications and operational reporting.

Payment coordination

Designed to support clearing, reconciliation, settlement and payout workflows with licensed banking and payment partners.

Architecture is not deployment status.

The capabilities above describe intended system support. Implementation, service availability and regulated functions depend on agreed phases, technical validation and relevant partner arrangements.
