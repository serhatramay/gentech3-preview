Clear scope for technical claims

Capability descriptions distinguish system design from live deployment. No group-wide payment, security, telecommunications or manufacturing certification is asserted on these pages.

Product-specific evidence

Certification, where relevant to a selected product, must be checked against the certificate holder, product and service scope, applicable market and validity period. A supplier’s certification is not a certification of Gentech Group.
