Enquiries on this preview

When you submit the form, your name, email address, optional company, selected topic, message and agreement to a response are transmitted over HTTPS to Gentech’s isolated form service hosted by Markum. The service sends the enquiry to info@gentech.ae using Markum’s mail service. Your email address is used as the reply-to address.

How your enquiry is handled

The receiving team can review your message, respond and coordinate with the relevant group team. This preview does not create a CRM record or send an automatic acknowledgement to the address you enter. A submission confirmation means the mail server accepted the message; it does not promise an immediate response. Do not send payment data, credentials or confidential documents.

Abuse prevention and technical records

The form service uses short-lived signed tokens, submission limits and pseudonymous request fingerprints to reduce unwanted and duplicate submissions. Its request-state file stores submission references, timestamps, outcomes and keyed hashes, not copies of your message or raw email address. Entries older than 24 hours are removed when subsequent submissions are processed. Normal hosting and mail-system logs may separately record connection and delivery information. The form does not use browser storage, analytics or advertising cookies.

Hosting and external resources

The preview pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts; the interactive card page loads its 3D library from Cloudflare’s CDN. These providers receive technical connection information when their resources are requested. Markum processes the form and email transmission. Information retained in the receiving mailbox is separate from the form’s temporary abuse-prevention records.

Questions about your information

Contact info@gentech.ae for questions or requests concerning your enquiry. This notice describes the test service; retention and organisational privacy arrangements require review before production launch.
